About the event

What is BrainByte?

BrainByte 2026 brings together students from every branch — coders, designers and idea people — for one full day 12 hour. Pick a problem statement, form a team of up to four, and ship something you're proud of by sunrise.

⏱️

12 Hours coding

One continuous 12-hour sprint with Wi-Fi, power backup.

🤝

Team Collaboration

Build solo or form a squad of 2–4 members. Don't have a team? Join our team-matching desk on day one and find your people.

🎓

Industry Mentors

Engineers from top product companies will float between tables to review your code, unblock bugs and sharpen your pitch.
